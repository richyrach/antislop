What you notice, and what we do about it

Most repairs start with a symptom, not a part name. Find yours.

Common bike symptoms and the repair for each
SymptomRepair
Tyre goes soft overnightFind the puncture, patch or replace the tube, check the tyre and rim tape for what caused it.
Brakes squeal, or the lever pulls to the barReplace worn pads, adjust or replace cables, bleed hydraulic brakes.
Chain skips or the gears jumpIndex the gears, replace a stretched chain, replace a worn cassette if needed.
Wheel wobbles as it spinsTrue the wheel, tighten or replace spokes.
Creak with every pedal strokeCheck pedals, crank, bottom bracket and headset, then clean and regrease what's dry.
Bike has been in a shed all winterFull service: clean, lube, check every bolt, brake, gear and tyre.
