Questions about seats and billing

What happens when a sixth person joins the Free plan?

You are asked to move to Team before the invitation is sent. Nothing in your workspace is changed or deleted.

What counts as a seat?

Each member who can create or edit tasks. Guests with comment-only access are not counted.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes, at any renewal date. Yearly plans are charged once, for twelve months of seats.

What happens to my projects if I move down to Free?

Projects beyond the three-project limit become read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can unlock them by upgrading again.
