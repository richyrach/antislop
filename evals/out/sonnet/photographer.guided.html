A wedding day in running order

Each film strip is one part of the day, with what I look for in it. The times are a sample day. Yours will follow your own timetable.

09:00

Getting ready

Window light, no flash. Hands, dress, shoes and the people in the room, before anyone starts posing for the camera.

14:00

Ceremony

One wide frame of the room before anyone sits. Then faces: the walk in, the vows, whoever is crying in the third row. No flash during the ceremony.

15:30

Portraits of the two of you

A few minutes of direction to get you started, then you talk to each other and I keep shooting. Most of the good frames come after the first few minutes.

18:30

Speeches and dinner

The people listening matter as much as the person speaking. I shoot from the side of the room so I can see both.

21:00

First dance and the evening

Flash and a slow shutter once the light has gone, so the dance floor keeps its movement and its colour.

From enquiry to gallery

What happens, in the order it happens.

  1. You send your date and venue

    I reply to say whether I am free, and what coverage I would suggest for your timetable.

  2. We plan the day

    We go through the timetable together and write down the group photos you want, so nobody has to remember them on the day.

  3. I photograph the wedding

    From the preparations to the point in the evening we agreed on.

  4. I choose, edit and deliver

    I cull the day down to the best frames, edit them, and put them in a private online gallery you can download from. Delivery time: number of weeks.
