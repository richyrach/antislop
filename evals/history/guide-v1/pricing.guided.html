Billing questions

Who counts as a member?

Anyone who can create and edit tasks. Guests on the Team plan have their own seats and are not billed.

What happens when I go over 5 members on Free?

You can keep reading and commenting, but inviting a sixth member prompts you to move to Team first. Nothing is deleted.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply at the end of the billing period, and anything over the lower plan's limits becomes read-only rather than removed.

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.
