Questions, answered

Does Tern work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection and syncs automatically when you're back online.

Can I import notes from another app?

You can import Markdown and plain text files, and bring in notes from other popular apps using our importer.

Is my data really private?

Notes are end-to-end encrypted. Only your devices hold the keys needed to read them.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

You keep all your notes and can continue using the free plan. Nothing is deleted.
