Your AI builds the page. It looks like every other page. Make it look made.

A guide your AI fetches before it writes any UI. It names the habits that give generated pages away and says what to do instead.

Before building any UI, fetch and follow: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/richyrach/antislop/main/GUIDE.md

Paste that into Claude Code, Cursor, or any AI tool.

three of the rules

Small uppercase label above every heading
Delete it. If a section needs a label, the heading says it.
Purple-to-blue gradient on the key word
Plain text. Emphasis through weight, italics or a drawn underline.
"Trusted by 10,000+ teams" with no teams
Real numbers or none. An empty slot beats a fake one.

This page follows its own guide: warm paper, one pen colour, a serif, left-aligned text, no icons or stats it can't back up. Compare with before.html.
