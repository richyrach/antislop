    Named after a bird that never stays put

    Tern takes its name from the Arctic tern, a seabird that breeds in the far north and spends the northern winter around Antarctica, then flies back.

    The drawing shows the two ends of that route. It is a sketch of the idea, not a map.

    What the demo pad does

    Everything in this table works on this page right now. Nothing you write leaves your browser.

    Actions in the demo pad
    ActionHow it behaves
    Write and saveEach note gets a date and time. Newest first.
    SearchFilters the list as you type, matching any part of a note.
    DeleteRemoves one note straight away. There is no undo.
    ExportDownloads every note as one plain text file.
    StorageKept in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data erases it.
