Hello, I'm Elara

Photography for people who'd rather be present than posed.

For over nine years I've photographed weddings the way I'd want mine remembered: unhurried, warm, and full of the small things — a shaking hand during vows, a grandmother's laugh, the quiet minute before the doors open.

My approach is part documentary, part gentle direction. I'll guide you just enough to feel comfortable, then step back and let your day unfold.
